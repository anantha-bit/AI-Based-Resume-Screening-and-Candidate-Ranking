Job Title: Data Analyst / Business Intelligence Specialist
Department: Business Intelligence & Operations
Location: Bengaluru, India

About the Role:
We are looking for a Data Analyst to transform complex datasets into actionable strategic business insights. You will design interactive dashboards, conduct exploratory data analysis, and evaluate A/B test experiments.

Key Responsibilities:
- Extract, clean, and analyze enterprise datasets using advanced SQL and Python (Pandas, NumPy).
- Design and maintain interactive executive dashboards using Power BI and Tableau.
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) to uncover trends and operational bottlenecks.
- Formulate and test statistical hypotheses to measure business performance and user retention.
- Present data-driven findings clearly to cross-functional stakeholders.

Minimum Qualifications (Required):
- Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, or Computer Science.
- 2+ years of professional experience in data analysis or business intelligence.
- Advanced SQL skills (joins, window functions, CTEs, aggregation).
- Strong proficiency in Python, Pandas, and NumPy for data manipulation.
- Extensive experience designing dashboards in Power BI or Tableau.
- Solid foundation in descriptive statistics and probability.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice to Have):
- Experience with Plotly, Seaborn, and Excel financial modeling.
- Exposure to cloud data warehouses such as BigQuery or Snowflake.
- Knowledge of machine learning basics and predictive modeling.
