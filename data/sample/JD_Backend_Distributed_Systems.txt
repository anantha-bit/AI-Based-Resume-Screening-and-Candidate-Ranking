Job Title: Backend Distributed Systems Engineer
Department: Core Platform Architecture
Location: Bengaluru, India

About the Role:
We are seeking a Backend Distributed Systems Engineer to design resilient, low-latency microservices handling millions of concurrent requests.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and maintain high-throughput backend services using Python, Go, or Java.
- Implement distributed caching and message streaming using Redis and Apache Kafka.
- Design robust database schemas and optimize complex SQL queries in PostgreSQL.
- Architect RESTful APIs and high-performance gRPC services.
- Ensure fault tolerance, distributed consensus, and load balancing across microservices.

Minimum Qualifications (Required):
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field.
- 3+ years of experience building distributed backend systems and microservices.
- Strong proficiency in Python, Go, or Java.
- In-depth experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and SQL query optimization.
- Hands-on experience with message brokers (Kafka or RabbitMQ) and caching (Redis).
- Solid knowledge of Git, Docker, and Linux environments.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice to Have):
- Experience with gRPC, Protocol Buffers, and WebSocket streaming.
- Understanding of distributed systems principles (CAP theorem, event-driven architecture).
- Experience with Kubernetes and CI/CD pipelines.
