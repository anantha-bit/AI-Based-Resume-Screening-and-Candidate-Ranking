Job Title: Senior Machine Learning Engineer
Department: Artificial Intelligence & Research
Location: Bengaluru, India (Hybrid)

About the Role:
We are seeking an experienced Senior Machine Learning Engineer to lead the design and deployment of modern GenAI and deep learning systems. You will architect transformer-based NLP models, fine-tune Large Language Models, and build low-latency retrieval pipelines.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, train, and deploy transformer architectures and LLMs for production NLP applications.
- Build and optimize Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines using vector databases (FAISS, Chroma).
- Lead end-to-end machine learning lifecycle from feature engineering to model monitoring.
- Collaborate with software engineers to integrate ML models into microservices using FastAPI and Docker.
- Mentor junior engineers and champion clean code and rigorous experimentation.

Minimum Qualifications (Required):
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, AI/ML, or related technical field.
- 4+ years of professional industry experience in machine learning and data science.
- Strong proficiency in Python, PyTorch, and Scikit-Learn.
- Demonstrated experience with Hugging Face Transformers, BERT, and Large Language Models.
- Deep expertise in Natural Language Processing (NLP), text embeddings, and vector similarity search.
- Solid understanding of SQL and relational databases.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice to Have):
- Experience with LangChain, LlamaIndex, or agentic GenAI frameworks.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP) and containerization (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Prior experience in prompt engineering, fine-tuning, and model distillation.
